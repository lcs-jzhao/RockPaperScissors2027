# Rock Paper Scissors
 
You against the computer. Click a move, and the computer picks one at random.
 
## How to play
 
1. Click **Rock**, **Paper**, or **Scissors**.
2. The computer shows its move beside yours.
3. Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock.
 
## What I added
 
- Pick the winner: decideWinner uses && and ||.
Colour the result: resultColor holds a Color, and the result text is green for a win, red for a loss and grey for a tie.
Best of five: the first to 3 round wins takes the match, the buttons turn off with .disabled, and a button appears to start again. Your list calls it “New Match”, and mine says “Play again”. You can rename it if you want it to match.
